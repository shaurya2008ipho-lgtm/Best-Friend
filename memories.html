Our First Meeting

We met for the first time when we both had to appear for the CRs interview. This was when our friendship began. After this, even I don't know, when you became an essential part of my world. How did I start caring for you, even I cannot figure out. When you are sad, I also remain sad. Anything that bothers you, it bothers me.

Maybe it happened in the little things—in the conversations that stayed with me, in the moments I started noticing when something wasn't quite right, and in how your happiness slowly began to feel like my own good news.

It is strange and beautiful how one ordinary meeting can become the beginning of something that matters so much. That day, we were simply two people showing up for an interview. I had no idea I was also meeting someone who would find a special place in my everyday world.

And if I could go back to that first day, I would walk into that room just the same—with no idea what was ahead, but with so much gratitude that life was about to introduce me to you. ♡

One of my favourite beginnings. Always.

And this is only the first page… ✧
